<h1> Bare Systems </h1>
</div>

Bare Systems builds practical open source tools for AI-first developers.

We are focused on software that helps small teams operate with leverage: local and self-hosted agent runtimes, deployment tooling, operator interfaces, edge systems, and security platforms that can be driven by both humans and AI.

## What We Build

- **Agent infrastructure** for running, routing, and operating AI systems in environments you control
- **Operator software** for managing services, devices, automation, and security workflows
- **Developer tooling** that makes shipping, debugging, and running distributed systems less fragile
- **Security platforms** for both defensive monitoring and offensive research in controlled environments

## Why We Exist

Bare Systems exists to build tools that are:

- open and inspectable
- self-hostable when it matters
- fast to understand and fast to operate
- useful to both individual builders and small serious teams

## Projects

Some of the work in this organization includes:

- **BearClaw**: a self-hosted AI agent runtime and automation core
- **Blink**: declarative deploy, verification, rollback, and reporting tooling
- **Tardigrade**: edge gateway and reverse proxy infrastructure
- **Koala**: local-first camera and home-state platform
- **Polar**: weather, climate, and environmental telemetry services
- **Kodiak**: trading and automation infrastructure
- **Ursa**: offensive and defensive security tooling

## Where We Are Going

We are building toward a cohesive stack of AI-native infrastructure: tools that work well on a laptop, in a homelab, or in production, and that stay understandable as they grow.

That means:

- tighter interoperability across projects
- stronger local-first and self-hosted workflows
- better operator visibility and control
- more automation without giving up auditability
- opinionated systems that remain hackable
